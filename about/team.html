Our People

Meet the People Behind TechMind

Our team brings together senior engineers from leading technology companies, experienced program managers, and organizational leaders who share a common mission: making technology education accessible to everyone.

Leadership

Our Team

TechMind Innovators is led by professionals from some of Canada's top technology companies and most respected organizations. Each member brings deep industry expertise and a genuine commitment to making digital skills accessible to communities across Canada and Latin America.

Andres Lozano

Founder & Executive Director Senior Software Engineer, Confluent

Andres founded TechMind Innovators with a clear vision: make the technical knowledge that engineers take for granted accessible to everyone. A senior software engineer at Confluent specializing in platform and infrastructure, Andres previously spent over three years on Amazon's Elastic Kubernetes Service (EKS) team, where he was among the founding engineers of the managed Kubernetes offering for AWS Outpost environments. He brings deep expertise in cloud infrastructure, distributed systems, and CI/CD to TechMind, personally designing and facilitating the organization's flagship workshops on web development and digital tools.

Edison Peña

Director of Applied Research Software Engineer, Amazon Web Services

Edison leads TechMind's applied research program, bringing a distinctive blend of computational science and software engineering. As a software engineer at Amazon Web Services, he builds cloud-native solutions and has developed generative AI tools using large language models and retrieval-augmented generation architectures. Prior to AWS, Edison conducted research at some of Canada's leading scientific institutions, including TRIUMF, where he engineered automation systems for particle accelerator diagnostics, and the Canadian Nuclear Laboratories, where he developed nuclear material analysis software. His research background in machine learning, signal processing, and data analytics gives TechMind a rigorous, evidence-based foundation for its educational programs.

Melike Bilgin

Director of Research, Programs & Stakeholder Engagement

Melike oversees the planning, coordination, and delivery of all TechMind research initiatives, programs, and stakeholder engagement efforts. She brings nearly four years of experience managing complex, multi-stakeholder initiatives at the BC Care Providers Association, where she led student recruitment, managed program budgets and grant funding, conducted research to inform program strategy, and built strong relationships with stakeholders across British Columbia. Her expertise in research coordination, program development, stakeholder relations, financial reporting, and community outreach ensures that every TechMind initiative runs smoothly and delivers measurable impact for the communities we serve.

George Alonge

Director of Technology & Infrastructure Staff Cloud Platform Engineer, SADA

George brings over a decade of experience in cloud engineering, site reliability, and infrastructure leadership. As a Staff Cloud Platform Engineer at SADA (An Insight company), he leads enterprise-scale implementations of infrastructure as code, Kubernetes deployments, and reliability engineering for major clients. George previously co-founded Liferithms, a lifestyle data analytics startup where he served as CTO, and held senior engineering roles at Fresh Prep, architecting cloud environments and CI/CD pipelines. His deep technical expertise in Terraform, Kubernetes, and scalable distributed systems strengthens TechMind's technology strategy and program delivery infrastructure.

Snezana Ristovski

Director of Strategy & Organizational Development

Snezana brings over 20 years of leadership experience in organizational development, operations management, and strategic planning. As the founder of a social impact and workforce development initiative, she has built programs that activate multigenerational talent while also delivering revenue-generating consulting and training solutions. Her experience has strengthened her governance expertise, stakeholder engagement, and strategic oversight skills. She is a recognized speaker on leadership, succession planning, and workforce development, and her innovative program design has earned a CSAE Award of Excellence. Previously, she held senior roles overseeing programs, innovation, and operations, managing stakeholder relationships, and leading interprovincial and international teams. Snezana's combined expertise in governance, program design, and strategic execution positions her to drive growth, innovation, and organizational effectiveness.

Community

Our Volunteers

Behind every TechMind workshop is a team of volunteer facilitators, mentors, and support staff who give their time to help others learn. Our volunteers come from across Canada's technology sector: software developers, UX designers, product managers, data analysts, and more. They share a common belief that everyone deserves the opportunity to build digital skills, and they are willing to spend their evenings and weekends making that happen.

If you are a tech professional looking for a meaningful way to give back, we would love to hear from you. No prior teaching experience is required. We provide training, resources, and a supportive community of fellow volunteers to help you get started.

Governance

Board of Directors

As a registered Canadian charity, TechMind Innovators is governed by a volunteer Board of Directors that operates in accordance with the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act and the requirements of the Canada Revenue Agency. Our board members serve without compensation and bring diverse professional backgrounds to their oversight role.

Andres Lozano

Board Chair & Founder Senior Software Engineer, Confluent

As the founder and Board Chair, Andres provides strategic leadership and ensures the organization stays true to its mission. His experience building platforms at Confluent and Amazon Web Services gives him a practitioner's perspective on the digital skills that matter most in today's workforce.

Snezana Ristovski

Board Director Governance & Organizational Development

Snezana brings extensive experience in program governance, risk management, and organizational strategy from senior leadership roles across British Columbia's healthcare and insurance sectors. She ensures TechMind's programs and operations are aligned with best practices in nonprofit governance.

George Alonge

Board Director Staff Cloud Platform Engineer, SADA

George provides technical oversight and infrastructure guidance to the board. His deep expertise in cloud platforms and enterprise-scale engineering helps TechMind make informed decisions about its technology strategy and digital program delivery.

Accountability

Board Responsibilities

Our Board of Directors is responsible for the overall stewardship of TechMind Innovators. The board meets quarterly to review organizational performance, approve budgets, set strategic priorities, and ensure compliance with all regulatory obligations.

Strategic Direction

Setting the organization's long-term vision and strategic priorities, approving major initiatives, and ensuring that all activities align with our charitable purpose of advancing technology education.

Financial Oversight

Approving annual budgets, reviewing quarterly financial statements, ensuring appropriate internal controls, and overseeing the annual audit process to maintain public accountability.

Regulatory Compliance

Ensuring that TechMind Innovators complies with all applicable laws, including the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act, CRA requirements for registered charities, and British Columbia provincial regulations.
