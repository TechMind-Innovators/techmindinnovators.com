Workshop

Build Your Website

Go from zero to a live, professional website in six hands-on sessions. No coding experience required — just bring your laptop and your business idea. Our software engineers will guide you through every step, from choosing a domain name to launching your finished site.

What You'll Learn

Each session builds on the last. By the end of the series, you will have a fully functional website that you own and can maintain independently.

01

Domain & Hosting

You will learn how to choose a domain name that works for your business, understand the difference between registrars and hosting providers, and set up a reliable hosting account. We walk you through the process of connecting your domain to your hosting so that when someone types your web address, they land on your site. By the end of this session, you will own a real domain name ready for your website.

02

WordPress Setup

WordPress powers over 40% of the web, and for good reason. You will install WordPress on your hosting account, configure essential settings like permalinks and security, and learn your way around the admin dashboard. We cover user roles, basic plugin management, and how to keep your installation up to date and secure. No coding required — WordPress handles the heavy lifting.

03

Design Principles

A good website is not just functional — it needs to look trustworthy and professional. You will learn fundamental design principles like visual hierarchy, colour contrast, and typography. We help you select and customize a theme that fits your brand, create a consistent layout across pages, and ensure your site looks great on both desktop and mobile devices.

04

SEO Basics

If people cannot find your website, it might as well not exist. You will learn how search engines discover and rank pages, how to write effective page titles and meta descriptions, and how to structure your content so that Google understands what your business does. We install and configure an SEO plugin and show you how to optimize every page you create.

05

Analytics Setup

You will install Google Analytics on your site and learn how to read the data it collects. We cover key metrics like page views, bounce rate, and traffic sources, and show you how to set up simple goals to track whether visitors are taking the actions that matter to your business — like calling your phone number, filling out a contact form, or viewing your menu.

06

Launch & Maintain

In the final session, we do a comprehensive pre-launch checklist: testing all links, checking mobile responsiveness, verifying load speed, and ensuring your site is secure with an SSL certificate. You will learn how to back up your site, update plugins safely, and handle common maintenance tasks. Then we launch your site together and celebrate.

Workshop Details

  • Duration: 6 sessions (one per week, approximately 2.5 hours each)
  • Format: In-person at the Vancouver Public Library — Central Branch
  • Prerequisites: None. If you can send an email and browse the web, you have enough skills to start. We handle the rest.
  • Cost: Free for eligible participants. TechMind Innovators is a registered Canadian charity, and this workshop is funded through donations and community partnerships.
  • What to bring: A laptop (we have a limited number of loaners available — let us know when you register), and your business name or idea.

Who This Is For

This workshop is designed for people who need a website but have never built one. You do not need to know how to code, and you do not need to be technical. If any of the following describe you, this workshop is a great fit.

Small Business Owners

You run a local shop, restaurant, cleaning service, consulting practice, or trade business in Metro Vancouver and you need a professional web presence to attract and serve customers. Your competitors have websites — now it is your turn.

Freelancers

You offer services like photography, graphic design, tutoring, translation, or bookkeeping and need a portfolio website to showcase your work and attract new clients. A professional site gives you credibility that social media profiles alone cannot provide.

Nonprofits

Your organization needs a website to share your mission, accept donations, recruit volunteers, and communicate with your community. We help you build a site that is functional, trustworthy, and easy to maintain with limited staff and budget.

Newcomers to Canada

You have recently arrived in Canada and are starting a business or offering services. Navigating the Canadian digital landscape can be overwhelming — from choosing a .ca domain to understanding local SEO. We provide patient, clear instruction in a supportive environment.
