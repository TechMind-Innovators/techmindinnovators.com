Volunteer

Share Your Skills, Shape the Future

Join a dedicated network of volunteers who are helping newcomers, small business owners, and underserved communities across Canada build the digital skills they need to thrive. Whether you have an hour a week or a day a month, your expertise can change lives.

Why Volunteer

Our volunteers are the backbone of TechMind Innovators. They bring real-world expertise into our workshops, mentorship programs, and digital literacy initiatives. Here is what volunteering with us looks like.

Make a Direct Impact

Work face-to-face with learners who are building websites, launching online businesses, or navigating digital tools for the first time. Your guidance helps participants overcome real barriers to digital participation and economic independence.

Build Your Network

Connect with a vibrant community of tech professionals, educators, nonprofit leaders, and community organizers across British Columbia and beyond. Many of our volunteers have formed lasting professional relationships through their time with TechMind.

Gain Teaching Experience

Develop your communication, facilitation, and mentorship skills in a supportive environment. Whether you are an experienced instructor or new to teaching, our team provides training and resources to help you succeed as a volunteer educator.

Flexible Commitment

We understand that your time is valuable. Volunteer on your own schedule — assist at a single workshop, commit to a monthly mentorship session, or contribute remotely by creating educational content. Every contribution matters.

Volunteer Roles

We offer a range of volunteer opportunities to match your skills and availability. Explore the roles below and find the one that is right for you.

Workshop Assistant

Support our facilitators during hands-on workshops by helping participants troubleshoot technical issues, follow along with exercises, and complete their projects. Workshop Assistants play a critical role in ensuring every learner gets the individual attention they need. No formal teaching experience is required — just patience, enthusiasm, and a solid foundation in web technologies or digital tools.

  • Time commitment: 2-4 hours per workshop
  • Location: In-person (Vancouver) or virtual
  • Skills: Basic web development, digital literacy

Mentor

Provide one-on-one guidance to program participants as they work toward their digital goals. Mentors are paired with learners based on shared interests and expertise, and meet regularly to offer advice, review progress, and provide encouragement. This role is ideal for experienced professionals who want to give back in a meaningful, relationship-driven way.

  • Time commitment: 1-2 hours per month
  • Location: Virtual or in-person
  • Skills: Industry experience, communication

Content Creator

Help us develop high-quality educational materials, tutorials, blog posts, and video guides that extend our reach beyond the classroom. Content Creators work with our programs team to identify knowledge gaps and produce resources that are accessible, practical, and culturally relevant for our diverse learner community.

  • Time commitment: Flexible, project-based
  • Location: Fully remote
  • Skills: Writing, design, or video production

How to Get Started

Getting involved as a volunteer is simple. Follow these three steps and you will be making an impact in no time.

1

Reach Out

Send us an email at programs@techmindinnovators.com with a brief introduction and the role you are interested in. Tell us about your background, skills, and availability.

2

Meet the Team

We will schedule a short orientation call to learn more about your interests, walk you through our programs, and match you with the right opportunity. We will also provide any training materials you need to get started.

3

Start Volunteering

Once you are onboarded, you will join our volunteer community and begin contributing to workshops, mentorship sessions, or content projects. Our team is always available to support you along the way.

Have questions before you apply? Reach out to our programs team at programs@techmindinnovators.com — we are happy to chat.

Ready to Make a Difference?

Your skills and experience can help someone build a website, launch a business, or take the first step toward a career in tech. Join our volunteer team and become part of a movement that is closing the digital divide across Canada.
