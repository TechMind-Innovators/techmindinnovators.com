Volunteer

Share Your Skills, Shape the Future

Join a dedicated network of volunteers who are helping newcomers, small business owners, and underserved communities across Canada build the digital skills they need to thrive. Whether you have an hour a week or a day a month, your expertise can change lives.

Why Volunteer

Our volunteers are the backbone of TechMind Innovators. They bring real-world expertise into our workshops, mentorship programs, and digital literacy initiatives.

Make a Direct Impact

Work face-to-face with learners building websites, launching online businesses, or navigating digital tools for the first time.

Build Your Network

Connect with tech professionals, educators, nonprofit leaders, and community organizers across British Columbia and beyond.

Gain Teaching Experience

Develop your communication, facilitation, and mentorship skills in a supportive environment with training and resources provided.

Flexible Commitment

Volunteer on your own schedule — assist at a single workshop, commit to monthly mentorship, or contribute remotely. Every contribution matters.

Volunteer Roles

We offer a range of volunteer opportunities to match your skills and availability.

Workshop Assistant

Support our facilitators during hands-on workshops by helping participants troubleshoot issues and complete their projects.

  • 2–4 hours per workshop
  • In-person (Vancouver) or virtual
  • Skills: Basic web development, digital literacy

Mentor

Provide one-on-one guidance to program participants as they work toward their digital goals.

  • 1–2 hours per month
  • Virtual or in-person
  • Skills: Industry experience, communication

Content Creator

Help develop tutorials, blog posts, and video guides that extend our reach beyond the classroom.

  • Flexible, project-based
  • Fully remote
  • Skills: Writing, design, or video production

How to Get Started

Getting involved as a volunteer is simple. Follow these three steps and you will be making an impact in no time.

1

Apply Below

Fill out the volunteer application form with a brief introduction and the role you are interested in.

2

Meet the Team

We will schedule a short orientation call to learn about your interests, walk you through our programs, and match you with the right opportunity.

3

Start Volunteering

Once onboarded, you will join our volunteer community and begin contributing to workshops, mentorship sessions, or content projects.
