Workshop

Find Your Customers

Learn the digital marketing fundamentals that actually work for small businesses. In four practical sessions, you will set up the tools, build the strategy, and start reaching the customers who are already looking for what you offer — no marketing degree required.

What You'll Learn

Each session focuses on a specific set of tools and strategies. You will leave every session with something configured, published, or scheduled — not just ideas, but real marketing assets for your business.

01

Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing potential customers see when they search for businesses like yours. You will claim or create your listing, optimize it with accurate categories, business hours, service areas, and photos, and learn how to respond to reviews professionally. For local businesses, this single free tool can drive more traffic than any paid advertising.

02

Social Media Strategy

You do not need to be on every platform — you need to be on the right ones. We help you identify where your customers actually spend their time, create a realistic posting schedule you can maintain, and develop content ideas that showcase your expertise without requiring a design team. You will set up or optimize your business profiles and schedule your first week of content before the session ends.

03

Email Marketing

Email remains the highest-ROI marketing channel for small businesses. You will set up a free email marketing account, create a signup form for your website, design your first email template, and learn the basics of segmentation and automation. We cover Canadian Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) compliance so you can build your list confidently and legally.

04

Content Creation

Great marketing starts with useful content. You will learn how to write compelling business descriptions, create blog posts that answer your customers' questions, and produce simple visual content using free tools like Canva. We focus on content that builds trust and demonstrates your expertise — the kind that turns browsers into buyers over time.

05

Paid Ads Basics

When you are ready to invest a small budget in advertising, you need to know how to spend it wisely. We introduce the fundamentals of Google Ads and social media advertising, including how to set a budget, target the right audience, write effective ad copy, and avoid common mistakes that waste money. You will not run a campaign in this session, but you will understand exactly how to when the time is right.

06

Measuring Results

Marketing without measurement is guesswork. You will learn how to read your Google Analytics reports, track which marketing channels drive actual customers (not just clicks), and set up a simple monthly reporting routine. We show you how to calculate your customer acquisition cost and make data-driven decisions about where to focus your limited time and budget.

Workshop Details

  • Duration: 4 sessions (one per week, approximately 2.5 hours each)
  • Format: In-person at the Vancouver Public Library with an online option available for participants outside Metro Vancouver
  • Prerequisites: An existing website is recommended but not required. If you do not have a website yet, consider taking our Build Your Website workshop first.
  • Cost: Free for eligible participants. This workshop is funded through donations and partnerships as part of TechMind Innovators' charitable mission.
  • What to bring: A laptop, login credentials for any existing business accounts (Google, social media, email provider), and a willingness to experiment.

Who This Is For

This workshop is built for people who know they need to market their business online but are not sure where to start or what is actually worth their time.

Small Business Owners

You run a local business in Metro Vancouver — a restaurant, salon, repair shop, accounting firm, or retail store — and you know your customers are searching online. You want to show up when they search, but you have never done digital marketing before or have tried and given up because it felt too complicated.

Service Providers

You offer professional services like consulting, coaching, cleaning, landscaping, or home renovation. Your business depends on a steady flow of new clients, and word of mouth alone is not enough anymore. You need a digital presence that works for you even when you are busy serving existing customers.

Local Retailers

You have a physical storefront and want to drive more foot traffic through digital channels. Whether you sell handmade goods, specialty food, clothing, or gifts, you will learn how to make your shop visible to people searching nearby and build a loyal customer base through email and social media.
