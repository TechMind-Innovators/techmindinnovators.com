Our People

Meet the Team Behind TechMind

Our team is made up of passionate software engineers, educators, and community organizers who volunteer their time and expertise to make technology accessible to everyone. Together, we design workshops, conduct research, and build the programs that power TechMind Innovators.

Our Core Team

TechMind Innovators is driven by a dedicated group of professionals who believe that sharing technical knowledge is one of the most impactful things they can do. Each member of our core team brings a unique blend of industry experience and a genuine commitment to community education.

MR

Marco Reyes

Lead Workshop Facilitator

Marco is a senior full-stack developer with over a decade of experience building web applications for startups and enterprise clients. He founded TechMind's workshop program and has personally facilitated more than 30 sessions on website development and digital tools. Marco specializes in breaking down complex technical concepts into clear, actionable steps that anyone can follow, regardless of their technical background.

SN

Sarah Nguyen

Programs Director

Sarah brings eight years of experience in nonprofit program management and a background in computer science. She oversees the design, scheduling, and delivery of all TechMind programs, ensuring that each one meets the real needs of our participants. Before joining TechMind, Sarah managed digital literacy initiatives at a settlement agency, giving her firsthand insight into the barriers newcomers face when navigating technology in Canada.

JK

James Kowalski

Research Lead

James leads TechMind's applied research efforts, studying digital adoption patterns and the effectiveness of community-based technology education. With a master's degree in information science and five years of experience as a data engineer, he brings both academic rigour and practical know-how to our research program. His work helps us understand where the biggest gaps in digital skills exist and how to address them most effectively.

AP

Amara Patel

Community Outreach Coordinator

Amara is the bridge between TechMind and the communities we serve. She manages partnerships with community centres, libraries, and local business associations across Metro Vancouver, and coordinates our outreach in Ecuador. With a background in social work and a passion for technology access, Amara ensures that our programs reach the people who need them most, particularly small business owners, newcomers, and underserved communities.

Our Volunteers

Behind every TechMind workshop is a team of volunteer facilitators, mentors, and support staff who give their time to help others learn. Our volunteers come from across Canada's technology sector: software developers, UX designers, product managers, data analysts, and more. They share a common belief that everyone deserves the opportunity to build digital skills, and they are willing to spend their evenings and weekends making that happen.

If you are a tech professional looking for a meaningful way to give back, we would love to hear from you. No prior teaching experience is required. We provide training, resources, and a supportive community of fellow volunteers to help you get started.

Want to Join Our Team?

We are always looking for skilled volunteers who want to share their technical knowledge and make a real difference in their community.
