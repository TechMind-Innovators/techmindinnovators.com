Community

Join the TechMind Community

Stay connected with TechMind Innovators and become part of a growing network of learners, builders, and change-makers across Canada. Get early access to workshops, free resources, and a supportive community that is passionate about digital inclusion.

What You Get

When you join the TechMind community, you gain access to a range of benefits designed to support your learning journey and keep you connected to the people and programs that matter most.

Workshop Announcements

Be the first to know when new workshops are scheduled. Community members receive email notifications about upcoming sessions — including our popular "Build Your Website" and "Find Your Customers" workshops — before they are announced publicly. Many of our workshops fill up quickly, so early notice gives you a real advantage.

Resource Library

Access our growing library of tutorials, guides, templates, and recorded workshop sessions. From step-by-step website building instructions to digital marketing checklists, our resources are designed to be practical, beginner-friendly, and available whenever you need them.

Peer Network

Connect with fellow learners, workshop alumni, mentors, and volunteers who share your interest in technology and digital skills. Our community includes newcomers to Canada, small business owners, career changers, and lifelong learners — all supporting one another on the path to digital confidence.

Priority Registration

Community members get priority access to register for workshops, mentorship programs, and special events before the general public. This ensures you can secure a spot in the programs that align with your goals, even when demand is high.

Sign Up

Joining the TechMind community is free and takes less than a minute. Fill out the form below and you will start receiving workshop announcements, resource updates, and community news directly in your inbox.

We respect your privacy. Your information will only be used to send you TechMind community updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Stay Connected

Beyond our mailing list, there are several ways to stay in touch with TechMind Innovators and follow our work.

  • Email: Reach our programs team anytime at programs@techmindinnovators.com. Whether you have a question about an upcoming workshop, need help accessing a resource, or just want to say hello, we are always happy to hear from you.
  • LinkedIn: Follow us on LinkedIn for professional updates, partner announcements, research highlights, and stories from our community. It is the best place to stay informed about our organizational milestones and industry insights.
  • Instagram: Follow us on Instagram for behind-the-scenes photos from our workshops, participant success stories, event recaps, and visual updates that capture the energy of the TechMind community.
  • GitHub: Explore our open-source projects, workshop code samples, and technical resources on GitHub. Whether you are a beginner looking at example code or a developer who wants to contribute, our repositories are open and welcoming.

No matter how you choose to connect, you are part of a community that believes technology education should be accessible, inclusive, and empowering for everyone.
