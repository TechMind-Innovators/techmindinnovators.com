Workshops

Master Complex Software Without the Overwhelm

Our workshops are led by experienced software engineers who break down powerful tools into clear, manageable steps. You will leave each session with something real — a live website, a marketing plan, a configured system — not just notes.

Current Workshop Series

We run focused workshop series throughout the year, each designed to take you from zero to confident in a specific digital skill. Here are the series currently accepting participants.

Build Your Website

A six-session series that takes you from zero to a live, professional website. You will register a domain, set up hosting, install WordPress, apply a theme, add essential pages, and learn the basics of SEO — all with hands-on guidance from our engineering team.

Learn More →

Find Your Customers

A four-session series focused on digital marketing fundamentals for small businesses. You will set up your Google Business Profile, create a social media strategy, learn email marketing basics, and understand how to measure what is actually working.

Learn More →

What Makes Our Workshops Different

There are plenty of online tutorials and YouTube videos out there. Here is why participants consistently tell us that our workshops succeed where self-study failed.

01

Led by Software Engineers

Our instructors are not marketing generalists reading from a script. They are professional software engineers, full-stack developers, and systems architects who build and maintain production software every day. When something breaks during a session — and it will — they can diagnose and fix it in real time, turning obstacles into teaching moments.

02

In-Person & Interactive

Most of our workshops take place at community venues like the Vancouver Public Library, where participants work on laptops side by side with instructors and fellow learners. There is no hiding behind a muted microphone. You get immediate feedback, can ask questions without waiting for a chat queue, and build connections with other business owners in your community.

03

Industry-Proven Methodologies

We teach the same tools and workflows used by professional development teams and digital agencies. WordPress with proper hosting and security, Google Analytics with structured event tracking, email marketing with segmentation and A/B testing — not watered-down versions, but the real thing, explained clearly for people encountering these tools for the first time.

04

Ongoing Support

The workshop is just the beginning. After each series, participants join our community where they can ask follow-up questions, attend monthly office hours, and access a growing library of step-by-step guides. If your website goes down at 10 PM on a Tuesday, you can post in our community forum and get help from someone who has been there.

All Workshops Are Free for Eligible Participants

TechMind Innovators is a registered Canadian charity. Our workshops are funded through donations, grants, and partnerships with organizations that share our mission. If you are a small business owner, newcomer to Canada, nonprofit operator, or entrepreneur in Metro Vancouver, you are likely eligible to attend at no cost. We believe that financial barriers should never stand between someone and the digital skills they need to succeed.
