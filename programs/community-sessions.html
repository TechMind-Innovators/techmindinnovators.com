Program

Community Sessions

An open online session every second Thursday evening, led by our engineers. Anyone is welcome, from individuals to small business owners. Come with your questions about the topic, and we will share everything we know.

Register by Email

To join a session, send us an email. We will reply to confirm your registration, then follow up by email with everything you need to join.

Register by Email →

At a Glance

When
Every Second Thursday
Starting around 5:30 PM (Pacific Time)
Length
About 1 Hour
Time for questions throughout
Format
Online
Live, led by our engineers
Who Can Join
Everyone
Individuals and small businesses
What to Expect

Ask Anything, Learn From Engineers

Led by Engineers

Each session is run by software engineers who work with these tools every day, and who explain them in plain language.

Bring Your Questions

There is no such thing as a basic question. Ask about anything related to the session's topic, whether it is for your business or for yourself.

We Share What We Know

Our engineers share everything they know about the topic: practical tips, the tools they use, and the mistakes to avoid.

Open to Everyone

You do not need any technical background. Individuals, entrepreneurs and small business owners are all encouraged to join.

How to Register

Three Simple Steps

1

Email Us

Send an email to programs@techmindinnovators.com with "Community Session" in the subject line. Tell us your name and, if you like, the question you want to bring.

2

Get Your Confirmation

We will reply by email to confirm your registration.

3

Join Us Online

We will follow up by email with the details you need to join the session on Thursday evening.

Have a Question You Have Been Stuck On?

Register by email and bring it to the next Community Session. Our engineers are happy to help.
