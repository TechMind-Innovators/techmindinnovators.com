Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Introduction

TechMind Innovators is committed to protecting your privacy. As a registered Canadian charity, we take the responsible handling of personal information seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, register for our programs, or otherwise interact with our organization.

By using our website or participating in our programs, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information in the following ways:

  • Newsletter subscriptions: When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address to send you updates about our programs, workshops, research, and community events.
  • Workshop and program registration: When you register for a workshop or program, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, organization name, and any relevant professional background information needed to tailor our programming.
  • Contact form submissions: When you reach out through our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and the content of your message.
  • Donation information: Donations made through our CanadaHelps page are processed by CanadaHelps, a third-party platform. We receive your name, email address, and donation amount for issuing tax receipts, but we do not collect or store payment card information.
  • Website analytics: We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized usage data, including pages visited, time spent on site, browser type, device type, and general geographic location. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify individual visitors.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Deliver programs and workshops: To register participants, communicate logistics, share materials, and provide follow-up support for our technology education programs.
  • Communicate about our work: To send newsletters, event announcements, workshop updates, and other relevant information you have opted in to receive.
  • Improve our services: To analyze website usage patterns, understand participant needs, evaluate program effectiveness, and enhance our offerings.
  • Issue tax receipts: To provide official donation receipts as required by the Canada Revenue Agency for registered charities.
  • Respond to inquiries: To reply to your questions, feedback, or requests submitted through our contact form or email.

Information Sharing

TechMind Innovators does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We will never share your data for commercial marketing purposes.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and delivering our programs, including:

  • Google Analytics: For anonymized website usage analysis and reporting.
  • Email platforms: To send newsletters and program communications to subscribers who have opted in.
  • CanadaHelps: For processing charitable donations securely.
  • Hosting providers: For website hosting and maintenance.

These service providers are contractually obligated to use your information only for the purposes we specify and to maintain appropriate security measures.

We may also disclose personal information if required by law, regulation, or legal process, or if necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of TechMind Innovators, our participants, or the public.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include secure data storage, encrypted communications where applicable, and restricted access to personal information on a need-to-know basis.

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to following industry best practices to safeguard your data.

Your Rights

As a Canadian organization, TechMind Innovators complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation. Under these laws, you have the right to:

  • Access your information: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct your information: Ask us to update or correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Withdraw consent: Opt out of receiving communications from us at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in our emails or contacting us directly.
  • Request deletion: Ask us to delete your personal information, subject to any legal obligations we may have to retain certain records.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at programs@techmindinnovators.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days, as required by applicable law.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and to collect anonymized analytics data. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality, such as navigation and access to secure areas of the site.
  • Analytics cookies: Used by Google Analytics to collect anonymized data about how visitors use our website, including which pages are most popular and how visitors navigate between pages. This information helps us improve our website and user experience.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information. Your continued use of our website and services after any changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our handling of your personal information, please contact us:

  • Email: programs@techmindinnovators.com
  • Location: Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada
  • Contact form: Contact Us page

We are committed to resolving any privacy-related complaints in a fair and timely manner. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
