Workshop

Build Your AI Agent

Create a working AI agent in one hour using n8n, the open-source workflow automation platform. Connect AI to your tools, automate decisions, and build something useful for your business — no coding required.

What You'll Learn

In one focused session, you will go from "what is an AI agent?" to having built a working one. Every activity is hands-on — you will leave with an agent you created, not just concepts you heard about.

01

What AI Agents Are & Why They Matter

Understand the difference between a chatbot and an autonomous AI agent. Learn how agents reason through tasks, use tools, and make decisions — and why this matters for automating real work in your business.

02

Visual Workflow Building with n8n

Get hands-on with n8n's drag-and-drop workflow canvas. Connect nodes, configure triggers, and build automation flows visually — no code required. See how n8n connects to over 400 apps and services your business already uses.

03

Build Your First AI Agent

Create a working AI agent that can reason, use tools, and take actions. Connect it to a language model, give it memory and goals, and watch it handle a multi-step task autonomously — all through n8n's visual interface.

04

Practical Use Cases for Your Business

Explore real-world applications: auto-responding to customer inquiries, summarizing incoming emails, generating reports from data, and routing support requests. Walk away with concrete ideas for agents that save you time every week.

Who This Is For

This workshop is for anyone who wants to put AI to work — automating tasks, handling decisions, and connecting their tools together.

Small Business Owners

You spend hours on repetitive tasks like responding to inquiries, sorting emails, or updating spreadsheets. An AI agent can handle that for you.

Operations & Admin Teams

You manage workflows across multiple tools and want to automate the handoffs. n8n connects your existing apps without replacing them.

Anyone Curious About AI Agents

You have heard about AI agents but want to see what they actually do. Build one yourself in an hour — no technical background needed.
