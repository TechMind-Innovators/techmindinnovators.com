Partnerships

Let's Scale Digital Education Together

TechMind Innovators partners with forward-thinking organizations to deliver technology education to the communities that need it most. Whether you are a corporation, a public institution, or a grassroots organization, there is a meaningful way to collaborate with us.

Partnership Types

We work with partners across sectors to fund, design, and deliver programs that make digital skills accessible to everyone.

Corporate Sponsorship

Align your brand with digital inclusion by sponsoring TechMind workshops, mentorship programs, or research initiatives.

  • Fund workshops and learning resources
  • Receive brand visibility at events and online
  • Engage employees through volunteer days
  • Receive annual impact and outcomes reports

Institutional Collaboration

We partner with universities, colleges, libraries, and government agencies to co-develop curricula and host workshops.

  • Co-develop curriculum and research projects
  • Host workshops in your facilities
  • Share data and insights on digital literacy
  • Collaborate on grant applications

Community Partnership

Grassroots organizations, settlement agencies, and local nonprofits are essential to reaching the communities we serve.

  • Connect us with underserved learners
  • Provide culturally appropriate learning spaces
  • Co-facilitate workshops and events
  • Inform program design with community insights

Current Partners

We are proud to work alongside organizations that share our commitment to digital equity and technology education.

Amazon Web Services (AWS)

Provides cloud computing credits and technical infrastructure that power our online learning platforms and workshop environments.

Vancouver Public Library

Hosts in-person workshops at library branches across Metro Vancouver, making our programs accessible in trusted community spaces.

Province of British Columbia

Supports our digital literacy programming through provincial grants that fund curriculum development and instructor training.

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)

Funds our newcomer-focused programs that help recent immigrants develop the digital skills needed to settle, work, and participate in Canadian society.

What Partners Get

Partnering with TechMind Innovators is a strategic investment in community development and digital equity.

Brand Visibility

Your organization featured on our website, workshop materials, social media, and event signage — reaching thousands of community members and stakeholders.

Impact Reports

Receive detailed quarterly and annual reports on program outcomes, participant demographics, and community impact with quantitative data and testimonials.

Tax Receipts

As a registered Canadian charity, TechMind Innovators issues official donation tax receipts for all eligible contributions per CRA guidelines.

Direct Community Impact

Your partnership directly funds workshops, mentorships, and digital resources for newcomers, small business owners, and underserved communities.
