Governance

Board of Directors

Strong governance is the foundation of public trust. Our Board of Directors provides strategic direction, financial oversight, and accountability to ensure that TechMind Innovators fulfills its charitable mission with integrity and transparency.

Our Commitment to Governance

As a registered Canadian charity, TechMind Innovators is governed by a volunteer Board of Directors that operates in accordance with the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act and the requirements of the Canada Revenue Agency for registered charities. Our board members serve without compensation and bring diverse professional backgrounds to their oversight role.

The board meets quarterly to review organizational performance, approve budgets and financial statements, set strategic priorities, and ensure compliance with all regulatory obligations. Board members also serve on committees focused on finance, programs, and governance, providing deeper engagement with the areas most critical to our mission.

We believe that good governance is not just a legal requirement but a moral one. Donors, participants, partners, and the public deserve to know that their trust is well placed. Our board works to uphold that trust in everything we do.

Meet Our Board

Our board members bring experience from across the technology, finance, education, and nonprofit sectors. Their combined expertise ensures that TechMind Innovators is well-positioned to grow sustainably while staying true to our mission.

DL

David Liu

Board Chair

David is a technology executive with over 15 years of leadership experience in Vancouver's software industry. He currently serves as VP of Engineering at a mid-size SaaS company and has a deep commitment to using technology as a force for social good. As Board Chair, David guides strategic planning and ensures alignment between the board's vision and the organization's day-to-day operations.

RH

Rachel Henderson

Treasurer

Rachel is a Chartered Professional Accountant with extensive experience in nonprofit financial management. She has worked with several charitable organizations across British Columbia, helping them build robust financial controls and reporting systems. As Treasurer, Rachel oversees TechMind's budgeting, financial reporting, and audit processes, ensuring that every dollar is accounted for and directed toward our mission.

KO

Kwame Osei

Secretary

Kwame is an education policy consultant who has spent his career working at the intersection of technology and learning. He has advised school boards, post-secondary institutions, and government agencies on digital literacy initiatives across Canada. As Secretary, Kwame ensures that board proceedings are properly documented and that governance policies remain current and effective.

Board Responsibilities

Our Board of Directors is responsible for the overall stewardship of TechMind Innovators. Key responsibilities include:

Strategic Direction

Setting the organization's long-term vision and strategic priorities, approving major initiatives, and ensuring that all activities align with our charitable purpose of advancing technology education.

Financial Oversight

Approving annual budgets, reviewing quarterly financial statements, ensuring appropriate internal controls, and overseeing the annual audit process to maintain public accountability.

Regulatory Compliance

Ensuring that TechMind Innovators complies with all applicable laws, including the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act, CRA requirements for registered charities, and British Columbia provincial regulations.

Transparency You Can Trust

We publish annual reports and financial statements so that our supporters can see exactly how their contributions are making an impact. Explore our latest reports to learn more.
