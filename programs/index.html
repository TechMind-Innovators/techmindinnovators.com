Programs

Programs That Meet You Where You Are

TechMind Innovators delivers four interconnected program pillars — interactive workshops, one-on-one mentoring, applied research, and AI workforce readiness — designed to help Canadian small businesses, newcomers, and individuals build lasting digital capabilities.

Our Four Pillars

Every program we offer is grounded in real-world needs. Whether you learn best in a group setting, prefer individual guidance, want to understand the data behind digital adoption, or need support navigating AI-driven workforce changes, we have a pathway for you.

Interactive Workshops

Hands-on, engineer-led sessions held at community venues across Metro Vancouver. Our workshops cover website building, digital marketing, inventory management, and more — all tailored for people who are new to these tools.

Explore Workshops →

One-on-One Mentoring

Personalized guidance from experienced software engineers and digital strategists. Mentoring sessions help you apply what you learn in workshops to your specific business context, tackling the challenges unique to your industry and goals.

Learn More →

Digital Adoption Research

We conduct applied research into how small businesses actually adopt new technology — from ERP systems like Odoo to cloud services and e-commerce platforms. Our findings are published openly and feed directly into better programs.

Visit Research Hub →

AI-Ready Workforce Program

As AI reshapes industries and job roles, many workers face uncertainty about their future. Through funded partnerships, we enrol individuals in targeted digital skills training, provide ongoing career support, and follow up with each participant to help them secure meaningful employment in the evolving economy.

How Our Programs Work

We follow a structured approach that ensures every participant gets the support they need — from initial assessment through to long-term follow-up.

01

Assessment

Every engagement begins with a digital readiness assessment. We evaluate your current technology usage, identify gaps, and understand your business goals. This helps us recommend the right combination of workshops, mentoring, and resources for your situation.

02

Personalized Path

Based on your assessment, we build a learning path that makes sense for your schedule and skill level. Some participants start with our Build Your Website workshop, while others jump straight into digital marketing or inventory management. There is no one-size-fits-all curriculum.

03

Hands-On Learning

Our workshops and mentoring sessions are built around doing, not just listening. You will set up real accounts, configure real tools, and build real assets for your business during each session. Every participant leaves with something tangible they can use immediately.

04

Follow-Up Support

Learning does not stop when the workshop ends. Participants gain access to our community forum, monthly office hours, and a library of step-by-step guides. If you get stuck configuring your website or running your first ad campaign, help is always available.

Who We Serve

Our programs are designed for Canadians who are ready to embrace digital tools but need practical, jargon-free guidance to get started.

Small Businesses

Local shops, restaurants, service providers, and micro-enterprises across Metro Vancouver. If you have fewer than 20 employees and want to get online, streamline operations, or reach more customers digitally, our programs are built for you.

Newcomers to Canada

Immigrants and refugees building new lives and livelihoods in British Columbia. We offer culturally sensitive instruction, multilingual support where possible, and a welcoming environment for people navigating the Canadian business landscape for the first time.

Nonprofit Organizations

Charities, community groups, and social enterprises that need to strengthen their digital presence. From building a donation page to managing volunteers with digital tools, we help nonprofits operate more effectively with limited budgets.

Entrepreneurs

Aspiring and early-stage founders who need to establish their digital foundation. Whether you are validating a business idea, setting up your first online store, or learning to measure what matters, we help you build smart from the start.

Workers Facing AI Disruption

Individuals whose jobs are being reshaped or displaced by artificial intelligence and automation. Through our AI-Ready Workforce Program, we provide funded digital skills training, career guidance, and employment follow-up to help people transition into new roles with confidence.
