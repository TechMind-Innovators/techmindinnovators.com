Community

Join the TechMind Community

Stay connected with TechMind Innovators and become part of a growing network of learners, builders, and change-makers across Canada. Get early access to workshops, free resources, and a supportive community that is passionate about digital inclusion.

What You Get

When you join the TechMind community, you gain access to benefits designed to support your learning journey and keep you connected.

Workshop Announcements

Be the first to know when new workshops are scheduled. Community members receive email notifications before public announcements — many of our workshops fill up quickly, so early notice gives you a real advantage.

Resource Library

Access our growing library of tutorials, guides, templates, and recorded sessions. From step-by-step website building instructions to digital marketing checklists, our resources are practical and beginner-friendly.

Peer Network

Connect with fellow learners, workshop alumni, mentors, and volunteers. Our community includes newcomers to Canada, small business owners, career changers, and lifelong learners — all supporting one another.

Priority Registration

Get priority access to workshops, mentorship programs, and special events before the general public. Secure a spot in the programs that align with your goals, even when demand is high.

Sign Up

Joining the TechMind community is free and takes less than a minute. Fill out the form below and start receiving workshop announcements, resource updates, and community news.

We respect your privacy. Your information will only be used to send you TechMind community updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Stay Connected

Beyond our mailing list, there are several ways to stay in touch with TechMind Innovators.

Email

Reach our programs team anytime at programs@techmindinnovators.com. Whether you have a question about a workshop or want to say hello, we are happy to hear from you.

LinkedIn

Follow us on LinkedIn for professional updates, partner announcements, research highlights, and stories from our community.

Instagram

Follow us on Instagram for behind-the-scenes workshop photos, participant success stories, event recaps, and visual updates.

GitHub

Explore our open-source projects, workshop code samples, and technical resources. Our repositories are open and welcoming to all skill levels.
