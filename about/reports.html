Transparency

Annual Reports & Financials

Accountability is at the heart of everything we do. We publish annual reports and financial summaries so that our donors, partners, and the public can see exactly how we use the resources entrusted to us.

Our Reports

Each year, we prepare a comprehensive annual report that documents our programs, impact, financials, and plans for the year ahead. These reports are reviewed and approved by our Board of Directors and are available for public download below.

2024

Annual Report 2024

Our most comprehensive year yet. This report covers the expansion of our workshop program, the launch of our research initiative, our first international programs in Ecuador, and a full financial summary for the 2024 fiscal year.

  • 28 workshops delivered across Metro Vancouver
  • 320 participants served in group workshops
  • Launch of one-on-one mentoring program
  • First research report published on digital adoption
  • International expansion to Quito, Ecuador

By the Numbers

Financial Highlights

We are committed to responsible stewardship of every dollar we receive. The majority of our funding goes directly to program delivery, including workshop materials, venue costs, and volunteer coordination. As a volunteer-driven organization, we maintain low overhead and maximize the impact of every contribution.

85% Funds Directed to Programs
10% Administration Costs
5% Fundraising Costs

These figures represent our target allocation ratios. Detailed financial statements, including audited revenue and expense breakdowns, are included in each annual report. Donors can also verify our charitable status and view our T3010 filings through the Canada Revenue Agency's public charity listings.

Regulatory Information

TechMind Innovators is a registered Canadian charity under the Canada Revenue Agency. Our charitable registration allows us to issue official donation receipts for income tax purposes. All donations made through our CanadaHelps page or directly to the organization are eligible for tax receipts.

Our annual T3010 Registered Charity Information Return is filed with the CRA each year and is available for public review through the CRA's charity listings. This return provides detailed information about our revenue, expenditures, activities, and governance.

We are incorporated under the Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act and are headquartered in Vancouver, British Columbia. Our bylaws, articles of incorporation, and governance policies are available upon request.
